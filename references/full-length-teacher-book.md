# Complete student-readable exam book protocol (v0.7)

## Non-negotiable delivery
- Preserve the original exam PDF, page-for-page, as the first section; never replace it with OCR retyping.
- Target >=20 pages of **substantive explanations excluding the original exam** for a full high-school chemistry exam when the material warrants it. Page count is a diagnostic, never a substitute for quality.
- Answerless exams: transcribe and verify every prompt/option/subquestion including figures; solve without a key, and do a separate check before publishing an answer table.
- Use the user's preferred Chinese teaching-book layout: navy hierarchical headings, red answers and key conclusions, clear formulas, readable spacing, question numbering and a topic index.
- For EACH option/subquestion: give the judgment or answer, the relevant prompt condition and an understandable reason. Correct the specific false statement. Provide the shared prerequisite lesson, derivations, worked variants, extensions and misconceptions at question level; do not repeat a miniature textbook under all four options.
- Apply `student-readable-explanations.md`: assume a little chemistry background. Explain actual content and unfamiliar terms at the point of use, but do not restart all of chemistry from first principles. Keep direct solving and deeper knowledge in separate reading layers. Scientific rigor is a correctness standard, not a requirement to use expert-facing prose.
- Maintain per-item statuses `verified`, `needs_visual_check`, `needs_chemistry_check`, `ambiguous`; never silently turn uncertain analysis into a final key.
- Check conservation of atoms, charge and electrons; quantitative results, units and significant figures; chemical plausibility and graphical interpretation.
- Render PDF pages and visually inspect. Do not claim verified/finished if any question remains unchecked. Do not fabricate missing detail to meet page count.
- Keep editable content and references in the repository; version the source, not only the PDF.
