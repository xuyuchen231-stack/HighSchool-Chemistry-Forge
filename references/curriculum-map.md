# Curriculum organization (generic, not an official chapter numbering)

## Required foundational content
1. Chemical language, amount of substance and stoichiometry
2. Matter classification, dispersions, electrolytes, ionic equations
3. Redox, electron transfer and conservation
4. Atomic structure, periodic table and periodic trends
5. Chemical bonding, intermolecular forces and structure-property links
6. Representative elements and their compounds
7. Reaction energy, rate and introductory equilibrium
8. Organic basics, functional groups and common transformations
9. Chemical experiments, safety, separation and quantitative measurement

## Selective compulsory depth
10. Reaction enthalpy, equilibrium constants, Le Chatelier, acid-base and solubility equilibria
11. Galvanic cells, electrolysis and corrosion
12. Electronic configurations, orbitals, hybridization, molecular geometry, crystals and unit cells
13. Organic nomenclature, isomerism, reactions, synthesis and characterization

Use a user-supplied textbook table of contents to map these topics to the precise edition and grade. The list is a starting taxonomy, not an assertion that every edition orders or labels them identically.
